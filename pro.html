Every lesson in Flutter Wizard is free, and stays free. Pro does not unlock a single one of them — there is nothing behind it that is knowledge.

What it actually buys

The free app asks you to stay online, because the ads between chapters are what pay for the lessons. An ad cannot serve without a network, so the connection is the price of the free tier being free.

  • Learn with no connection. The whole curriculum already ships inside the app; Pro is what lifts the requirement to be online.
  • No ads, anywhere. Nothing between chapters, nothing to dismiss.
  • The whole arcade from day one, without waiting on a lesson count.
  • Progress on every device — your path, streak and mastery follow you.
  • Teardowns in full, every screen of every real-app rebuild.
  • It keeps the lessons free for everyone else.

Buying it

Pro is a yearly subscription. It renews automatically until you cancel, and you can cancel any time from your store account settings — deleting the app does not cancel it.

Buy it inside the app, on the You → Wizard Pro screen. Checkout on this page is not open yet; when it is, this page will take the payment directly and the account you are signed in with will be upgraded as soon as it clears.

Refunds

A subscription bought through the App Store or Google Play is refunded by them, under their own policies — we cannot process those, and asking them directly is faster than asking us.

Apple: report a problem at reportaproblem.apple.com. Google: Play Store → Menu → Subscriptions.

Questions about Pro, or a refund? Email hello@flutterwizard.app.
We answer within seven days.
